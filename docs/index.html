THE RESEARCH QUESTION

What does expert gaze contribute?

GazeRefine uses fixation-derived spatial weights to build foreground and background prototypes from frozen DINOv3 patch features. Prototype contrast, feature-affinity propagation, and anchored refinement turn these prototypes into segmentation scores at test time.

The controlled study separates image-specific gaze from center bias, fixation-duration weighting, background cleaning, and kNN propagation. Further experiments examine partial scanpaths and gaze-coordinate noise on polyp images and prostate MRI slices.

01

Location matters

Matched expert fixations outperform the tested center and shuffled-gaze controls on both datasets.

02

Background contrast matters

Removing background cleaning produces the largest measured component drop in the factorial study.

03

More gaze has diminishing gains

The first 75% of the fixation sequence recovers mean Dice within 0.2 points of full scanpaths.

METHOD

From attention to anatomy.

A frozen representation, organized around an image-specific target cue.

01 / SPATIAL PRIOR

Represent the gaze

Gaussian kernels map fixation locations and relative durations onto the patch grid.

Fixations → gaze map
02 / PROTOTYPES

Separate the target

Gaze and complementary weights pool foreground and background features. Their contrast suppresses shared responses.

Frozen features → FG / BG
03 / REFINEMENT

Extend the response

kNN feature affinities propagate scores; recurrent updates remain anchored to the gaze prompt.

Contrast → mask

CONTROLLED ABLATIONS

The prompt and the inference both matter.

Four gaze conditions × kNN on/off × background cleaning on/off.

Duration weighting is a small, dataset-dependent effect.

Equal-weight expert gaze gives 87.75% on Kvasir-SEG and 80.62% on NCI–ISBI, versus 88.10% and 80.28% with the tested duration weighting. The analysis supports the value of fixation locations more strongly than the particular duration transform.

GAZE ROBUSTNESS

What happens when gaze is imprecise?

Coordinate perturbations at 1%, 2.5%, 5% and 10% of image width and height.

1% COORDINATE NOISE

88.03 / 80.04

Kvasir-SEG / NCI–ISBI mean Dice. Both remain close to the unchanged-gaze reference.

10% COORDINATE NOISE

79.65 / 66.13

Larger perturbations degrade segmentation, with a greater drop on the evaluated prostate collection.

These experiments test synthetic coordinate noise while keeping fixation durations, ordering and model settings fixed. They do not validate a real eye tracker’s calibration, webcam accuracy or clinical performance.

INTERPRETATION

What this study establishes—and what remains open.

Supported by the controlled experimentsImage-matched gaze provides useful target information; background contrast is influential; and scanpath and noise analyses reveal sensitivity to the prompt.

Still to be establishedClinical utility, prospective stopping rules, independent-observer generalization, and the accuracy of consumer webcam gaze. The desktop application is a research preview.
